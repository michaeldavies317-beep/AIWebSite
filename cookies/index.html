Cookies

We keep this light. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. We count visits with a cookie-free tool, and the only things we store on your device are the ones the site needs to work, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

Last updated 27 November 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar way of saving a little information on your own device. The law treats them the same way, so we list both here.

All of these are "strictly necessary", which means the site or a service you asked for can't work without them. The law doesn't require us to ask permission for these.

Counting visits, without cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages are popular and roughly where visitors come from (for example a Google search, and which country). It doesn't set cookies or use local storage, doesn't build a profile of you, doesn't follow you to other websites and doesn't keep your IP address. A small script sends the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device type and how quickly the page loaded. We only ever see totals, never individual people. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare's privacy policy. If you'd rather not be counted, a content or tracker blocker in your browser will stop it.

Other websites we send you to

  • Stripe. When you pay, you go to a Stripe checkout page. Stripe uses its own cookies there to keep payments safe and stop fraud. See Stripe's cookie information.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load their fonts from Google. This doesn't set cookies, but Google does see your IP address when the fonts load. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Web3Forms. Our forms are sent through Web3Forms. It doesn't set cookies on our site.

Controlling cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. If you block them, the members sign-in won't work, and Pip may lose count of your free questions.

If we ever add cookies or anything else on your device that isn't strictly necessary, we'll update this page and ask for your permission first.
