Privacy policy

The short version: we only collect what we need to help you, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Last updated 27 November 2026.

What we collect and why

  • Contact, quote and sign-up forms. Your name, email address, the topic you pick and your message, so we can reply, send a quote or let you know when we launch. Forms reach us by email through a service called Web3Forms.
  • Emails you send us. If you email any of our addresses (hello@, business@, support@, parents@, orders@ or mike@), we receive your email address and whatever you write. Our email is forwarded by Cloudflare and stored in Google's Gmail.
  • Questions you ask Pip, our chat helper. We save the question, the date, which part of the site you were on, and whether you'd used up your free questions. We don't save your name, IP address or anything that links the question to you. Anything that looks like an email address or phone number is blanked out before it's saved. The questions are stored in a Cloudflare database and deleted automatically after 12 months. To write Pip's answers, your question and the last few messages of your chat are sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models. To enforce the 3 free questions a day, we keep a scrambled code made from your IP address and the date. It can't be turned back into your IP address, and it's deleted after 2 days. If you use Pip's quote helper, your answers are sent to Anthropic to run the chat, and reach us by email (through Web3Forms) only when you press Send with your name and email. Quote helper chats aren't saved in the question log. Please don't type personal details into Pip.
  • Payments. Stripe handles all payments. We see your name, email address, billing country, what you bought and when, and whether a subscription is active. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Members area. If you buy a plan or a guide, we add your email address to a members list so you can sign in with a one-time code. Cloudflare sends the code and records when you sign in.
  • Emails about what you bought. When you buy a plan or a guide, we send you a welcome email with how to log in and where to start, and add your name and email address to the matching mailing list (for example "Plus members" or "Parent guide buyers"), so we can tell you when new content for your plan or guide is added. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing never affects your access. When a plan ends, we take you off that plan's mailing list. Brevo sends these emails for us and tells us whether they were delivered, opened or clicked.
  • Support and sessions. If you're a member or book a session, we keep notes of what you've asked for help with, so we don't have to ask you twice.
  • Business work. If you ask us for a quote or hire us, we keep your contact details, your company name and the details of the work.
  • On your device. The site remembers how many free Pip questions you've used today, stored in your own browser. Members get a sign-in cookie. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Our cookie notice lists everything.
  • Visitor numbers. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies, doesn't keep your IP address and doesn't follow you to other sites. It tells us totals only, such as how many visits a page had, which websites sent visitors and which countries they came from. We can't tell who you are from it.

Our lawful reasons

UK data protection law says we need a lawful reason for each use of your data. Ours are:

  • Contract: to provide something you've bought or asked us for, such as a plan, a guide, a session or a quote.
  • Legitimate interests: to answer messages, to keep the site secure, to count visits (without cookies) so we know which pages help people, and to use anonymous Pip questions to write better tips and guides. We've checked that this doesn't override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: to keep payment and tax records.
  • Legitimate interests (soft opt-in): to email customers about new content for the plan or guide they bought, as UK law (PECR) allows for existing customers. You can unsubscribe from any of these emails with one click.
  • Consent: to email you about our launch or news, if you asked us to. You can say stop at any time and we'll remove you.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted companies to run the site. They only use your data to provide their service to us.

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, email forwarding, the members sign-in, the Pip question database and cookie-free visitor counts (Web Analytics).
  • Anthropic: writes Pip's answers using its Claude AI model.
  • Web3Forms: delivers our website forms to our inbox.
  • Stripe: takes payments and manages subscriptions.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): sends our welcome emails and "New this month" emails, and keeps our customer mailing lists. Its data is stored in the EU.
  • Google: our email (Gmail) and the website fonts (Google Fonts). When a page loads, your browser fetches the fonts from Google, so Google sees your IP address.

Some of these companies store or process data outside the UK, for example in the USA. When that happens it's protected by the legal safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK-US "data bridge" or the ICO's standard contract clauses.

We never sell your data, and we only share it with anyone else if the law requires it.

Children

Our services are for adults. Our parent guides are written for parents and carers to use with their children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their details, tell us and we'll delete them.

Keeping it safe

We use well-known services with strong security, protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-step sign-in, and keep access to your data to us alone. No system is perfect, but if something ever went wrong that put you at risk, we'd tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your data (unless we have to keep it by law, such as payment records)
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it
  • give you your data in a format you can take elsewhere

Just email hello@easystartai.co.uk. It's free and we'll reply within one month. We don't make any decisions about you by computer alone.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If it's a big change that affects you, we'll email you.
