Privacy

We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to help you, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Easy Start AI is run by Mike in the UK. Mike is responsible for your data. Contact: hello@easystartai.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Contact and sign-up forms. Your name, email and message, so we can reply to you. Forms are sent to us by email through Web3Forms.
  • Questions you ask Pip. We save the question, the date and which part of the site you were on, so we can see what people want to know and write better tips and guides. We don't save your name, IP address or anything that links the question to you. Anything that looks like an email address or phone number is blanked out before it's saved. Please don't type personal details into Pip. These questions are deleted after 12 months.
  • Payments. Stripe handles payments. We see your name, email and what you bought, but never your card details.
  • Members area. We keep your email on a members list so you can sign in with a one-time code. Cloudflare sends the code.
  • On your device. The site remembers how many free Pip questions you've used today, stored in your own browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our lawful basis

We use your details to answer you and to provide what you've paid for (contract and legitimate interests). We keep payment records because the law requires it.

Who helps us

Cloudflare (hosting, email forwarding and sign-in), Web3Forms (forms), Stripe (payments) and Google (email). Some of these may process data outside the UK, under the usual legal safeguards.

How long we keep things

Messages: up to 2 years after we last spoke. Members list: while you're a member, then 6 months. Payment records: 6 years (HMRC rules). Pip questions: 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email hello@easystartai.co.uk and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
