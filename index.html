For parents

Helping your kids use AI safely, without the panic.

Kids are already using AI for homework, games and chat. That's not a bad thing. With a few sensible settings and a couple of good conversations, you can help them use it well.

Get the free checklistSee the guides

The three things that matter most

1

Know what they use

Which apps and chatbots, on which devices. Most have age settings you can switch on.

2

Set things up together

Turn on family controls and talk through the settings with them, not behind their back.

3

Keep talking

Agree a few simple rules, like never sharing personal details, and check in now and then.
