CV and job application kit

Refresh your CV, write a cover letter that fits the job, and practise for the interview. Take it one step at a time over an evening.

Privacy first: take your address, phone number and email off your CV before you paste it in. Add them back at the end.

1. Refresh your CV

Here's my CV: [paste]. I'm applying for [type of role] in the UK. Suggest improvements: a stronger opening summary, clearer bullet points that show results, and anything that's missing or out of date. Keep it to two pages. Don't invent anything.

2. Match it to the job

Here's the job advert: [paste]. Compare it with my CV. Which skills do they ask for that I already show? Which are missing or hidden? Suggest small, honest changes to fit this role.

3. Write the cover letter

Write a cover letter for this role using my CV. Three short paragraphs: why this company, what I'd bring (two real examples), and a friendly close. Under 300 words, in British English.

4. Practise the interview

Act as the interviewer for this role. Ask me one question at a time, mixing common questions with ones specific to the advert. After each answer, give me one thing I did well and one thing to improve.

5. The tricky questions

Help me answer these honestly and positively: [for example, a gap in my CV, why I'm leaving my job, salary expectations]. Give me a short answer I can say in my own words.

Before you send: read every line. Recruiters can spot generic AI writing, and you need to be able to talk about everything on your CV. If it's not true, take it out.
