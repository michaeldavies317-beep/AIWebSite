Money and budgeting with AI

Turn a month of spending into a simple budget you understand, without sharing anything sensitive.

The golden rule: never give an AI app your bank login, card numbers or account numbers. You won't need them. AI is a helper with sums and ideas, not a financial adviser.

Step 1: make a safe list of your spending

Look through last month's statement and write a simple list: what it was and how much. Round to the nearest pound. Shop names are fine; leave out everything else. Or download your statement as a spreadsheet and delete every column except date, description and amount.

Step 2: sort it

Here's my spending for last month: [paste list]. Sort it into categories (bills, food, transport, fun, subscriptions, other) and give me a table with a total for each, plus the overall total.

Step 3: find the easy wins

Looking at this, what are three realistic ways I could save £[amount] a month without making life miserable? Point out any subscriptions that look forgotten.

Step 4: build a simple budget

My take-home pay is about £[amount] a month. Using the categories above, suggest a simple monthly budget that leaves £[amount] for savings. Show it as a table I can copy into a spreadsheet.

Step 5: check in each month

Here's this month's spending: [paste]. Compare it with my budget: [paste]. What went well, what went over, and one thing to try next month?

For bigger decisions (debts, pensions, mortgages), free, impartial help is available from MoneyHelper, which is backed by the government.
