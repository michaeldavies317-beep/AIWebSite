Notes to actions

Turn rambling voice notes, meeting scribbles and "I must remember to..." into a tidy to-do list. Great if you think out loud.

1. Capture it your way

Most AI apps let you talk instead of type (look for the microphone). Or use your phone's voice notes and paste the transcript. Don't worry about being tidy; ramble away.

2. Turn a ramble into a list

Here's me thinking out loud: [paste or speak]. Turn it into a to-do list. Group the items, put the most urgent first, and flag anything that needs someone else.

3. After a meeting or call

Here are my notes from a meeting about [topic]: [paste]. Give me a short summary, the decisions made, and a list of actions with who's doing each one. Then draft a short follow-up email to everyone.

4. Plan your week

Here's my to-do list: [paste] and my week looks like this: [busy days and free time]. Suggest when to do each item, keeping Friday afternoon light.

5. Make it a habit

Keep one chat called "My notes". Every evening, add the day's thoughts and ask:

Add today's notes to my list and tell me the three things to do first tomorrow.

Work meetings: check your workplace's rules before recording or pasting meeting notes into an AI app, and always tell people if you're recording.
