Your first AI assistant for work

Set up an AI helper that already knows your job, so you stop explaining yourself every time. It takes about 30 minutes.

1. Write your "about me" note

Most chatbots let you save instructions that apply to every chat (look for custom instructions, personalisation or a project). Paste in something like:

I'm a [role] at a [type of business] with [number] staff. Our customers are [who]. I write in a [friendly / formal] tone and use British English. Keep answers short unless I ask for detail. Never invent facts: say when you're not sure.

2. Add your three most common jobs

The jobs I'll ask you for most are: 1) [e.g. replying to customer emails], 2) [e.g. writing quotes], 3) [e.g. summarising meetings]. For each, here's an example I'm happy with: [paste].

3. Give it your house style

  • Your email sign-off
  • Words you always or never use
  • A short note on your prices or services, if it's not confidential

4. Test it on a real task

Ask it to do one of your three jobs with today's real work. Then correct it: "Shorter", "Less formal", "Don't mention prices". Update your note with anything you had to correct twice.

Keep it safe: don't paste customer personal data or anything confidential unless your AI tool is on a business plan that keeps your data private.
