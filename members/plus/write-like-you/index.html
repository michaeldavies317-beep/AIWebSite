Teach AI to write like you

Fed up with AI that sounds like a robot? In about 40 minutes you'll have a saved "voice guide" that makes every draft sound like you.

Step 1: gather three examples

Find three things you've written that you're happy with: an email, a social post, a card message. Real ones work best. Remove anything private.

Step 2: let AI study them

Here are three things I wrote: [paste them]. Describe my writing style in a short bullet list: tone, sentence length, words I use a lot, how I start and finish, and anything I never do. Keep it under 12 bullets.

Step 3: check and tweak

Read the list. Is it right? Fix anything that isn't you, for example "I never use exclamation marks" or "I always say 'cheers' not 'thanks'".

Step 4: save it

Copy the final list into your app's saved instructions (look for custom instructions, personalisation or a project). Start it like this:

When you write anything for me, follow my voice guide: [paste your list]. If something I ask for wouldn't fit my style, say so.

Step 5: test it

Using my voice guide, write a short message to [who] about [what].

If it's not quite right, tell it exactly what's off ("too formal", "I'd never say 'delighted'") and add that to your guide.

Good to know: keep it honest. It's fine to use AI to help you write, but if a piece really needs to be your own words (a personal apology, a school application), use the draft as a starting point and rewrite it yourself.
