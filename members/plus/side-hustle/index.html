Start a side hustle with AI

Got a skill, a hobby or a bit of spare time? Use AI to go from "maybe one day" to your first listing and a week of posts.

1. Find the idea

I'm good at [skills] and enjoy [hobbies]. I have about [hours] a week and a budget of £[amount]. Suggest five small side hustle ideas I could start from home in the UK, with how each one makes money and a realistic first step.

2. Test it before you spend

For [chosen idea], who would buy this and why? Suggest three cheap ways to test whether people want it before I spend any money.

3. Name it

Suggest 10 short, friendly names for [idea]. Avoid anything that sounds like a big brand. Then help me check which are easy to spell and say out loud.

Always check a name isn't already taken, for example with a quick web search and the free Companies House search.

4. Write your first listing

Write a product listing for [item or service] for [Etsy / Vinted / Facebook Marketplace / my own page]. Include a catchy title, a short description, three key benefits and a price suggestion based on [your costs].

5. A week of social posts

Write seven short social media posts for [business name], one a day for a week. Mix: one behind the scenes, two showing the product, one tip, one customer question, one offer, one about me. Add a picture idea for each.

6. The boring but important bits

I'm starting [idea] as a side hustle in the UK. In plain English, what should I know about telling HMRC, the trading allowance, insurance and keeping records? Keep it short and tell me where to check the official details.

Check the rules: AI gives a helpful overview, but tax and legal rules change. Confirm anything important on GOV.UK.
