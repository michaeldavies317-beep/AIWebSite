Inbox clear-out: 5 email templates

Copy a prompt, fill in the [brackets], and paste it into your AI chat. Always read the reply before you send it.

1. The polite chase

Write a short, friendly email chasing [person] about [thing] that was due on [date]. Keep it warm, not pushy. Under 80 words.

2. Saying no nicely

Help me politely decline [request] from [person]. I want to keep a good relationship and, if possible, suggest [alternative].

3. The tricky reply

Here's an email I received: [paste]. I feel [how you feel]. Draft a calm, professional reply that [what you want to happen].

4. Turn notes into an update

Turn these rough notes into a clear update email for [audience], with a short summary first and any actions at the end: [paste notes]

5. The "sorry for the delay"

I'm replying late to [person] about [topic]. Write a brief, genuine apology and then answer their question: [paste question].

Tip: keep private details out. Swap real names and account numbers for "Customer A" or "[account]" before you paste.
