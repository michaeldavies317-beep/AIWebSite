Starter

Plan a trip or day out

From a rainy Saturday to a week away: one chat for ideas, a plan, a budget and a packing list.

Step 1: ideas

Suggest five ideas for a [day out / weekend / week away] from [town or city] for [who's going, with ages]. We like [things you enjoy] and our budget is about £[amount]. Include one idea that works if it rains.

Step 2: the plan

Let's go with [idea]. Make a simple plan for [dates or day], with rough times, travel between places and a lunch stop. Keep it relaxed, not packed.

Step 3: the budget

Make a rough budget table for this trip: travel, tickets, food and extras, with a total. Flag anything I should book in advance to save money.

Step 4: the packing list

Write a packing list for this trip as a checklist, grouped by person. The weather is likely to be [weather]. Don't forget chargers and anything for the journey.

Check before you go: AI can be out of date on opening times, prices and events. Always check the official website before you book or set off.
