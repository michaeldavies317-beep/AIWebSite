Start here: your first week with AI

Five days, about ten minutes a day. Each day you'll get one small, real win. No tech skills needed, just a free AI chat app such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Before day 1: pick one app and sign in. Not sure which? Our cheat sheet helps you choose in two minutes.

Day 1: Just say hello

Treat it like a friendly, very well-read assistant. Ask it something you've wondered about this week.

Hi! I'm completely new to this. Explain what you can help with in everyday life, in five short bullet points.

Today's win: you've had your first conversation. That's the hardest bit done.

Day 2: Ask it to explain something

Pick a thing you've never quite understood: a word in the news, a setting on your phone, how a mortgage works.

Explain [topic] to me like I'm a clever 12-year-old. Use one everyday example. Then check I've understood by asking me one question.

Today's win: you've learned that you can ask follow-up questions, as many as you like.

Day 3: Write something you've been putting off

A message to a friend, a note to school, a review, a short email. Let AI do the first draft, then make it yours.

Help me write a short, friendly message to [who] about [what]. Keep it under 60 words. Give me two versions: one casual, one a little more polite.

Today's win: you've seen that AI drafts, and you decide. Always read before you send.

Day 4: Get organised

Give it a messy list from your head and let it sort it out.

Here's everything on my mind this week: [type it all out, any order]. Sort it into today, this week and can wait. Suggest the one thing I should do first.

Today's win: a clear head and a plan, in two minutes.

Day 5: Make it yours

Most apps let you save a few lines about yourself so you don't have to repeat them. Look in Settings for personalisation, custom instructions or memory.

I live in the UK and use British English and pounds. I'm a beginner with AI, so keep answers short and plain, and ask me a question if you're not sure what I mean.

Today's win: from now on, answers will fit you better.

Three habits to keep

  • Give it context. Who you are, what you want, and how you want it. Our prompt recipe card shows you how.
  • Check what matters. AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Double-check anything about health, money or the law.
  • Keep private things private. Leave out passwords, bank details and other people's personal information.
