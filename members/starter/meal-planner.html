A week of meals in 10 minutes

One prompt, one week of meals and a shopping list. Adjust it once, then reuse it every week.

Step 1: tell it about your household

Plan 5 evening meals for [number] people. We like [foods] and avoid [foods/allergies]. Weeknight meals should take under [30] minutes. Budget is about £[amount] for the week.

Step 2: use what you've got

We already have [list what's in the fridge and cupboards]. Use as much of it as you can.

Step 3: get the shopping list

Now give me one shopping list grouped by supermarket aisle, with rough quantities. Leave out what we already have.

Step 4: save it for next week

Next week, open the same chat and say: Same again, but swap two meals and include one new recipe.
