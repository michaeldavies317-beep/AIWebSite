Starter

Make sense of any letter or form

Bills, payslips, letters from the bank or a form full of jargon: AI can turn them into plain English and tell you what, if anything, you need to do.

First, cover up the private bits. Before you paste or photograph anything, hide account numbers, your National Insurance number, your address and signatures. AI doesn't need them to explain the letter.

Step 1: get the text in

Either type out the confusing part, or take a photo (most AI apps have a paperclip or camera button). A clear, flat photo in good light works best.

Step 2: ask for plain English

Explain this letter to me in plain English. Start with one sentence on what it's about. Then tell me: Do I need to do anything? By when? What happens if I don't?

Step 3: dig into the confusing words

What do these terms mean, in simple words, with an everyday example: [list the words].

Handy versions

Payslip:

Here's my payslip with the private details hidden. Explain each line, what tax code [code] usually means, and anything that looks unusual compared with a normal UK payslip.

Energy bill:

Explain this energy bill. How much did I use, what's the standing charge, and is this an estimate or a real reading? What questions should I ask my supplier?

A form:

I need to fill in this form: [paste the questions]. Explain what each question is really asking, and what information I should gather before I start.

Remember: AI explains, it doesn't decide. If a letter is about money you owe, a benefit or legal action, check with the organisation that sent it, or with Citizens Advice.
