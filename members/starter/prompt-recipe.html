The prompt recipe card

Good answers come from good questions. This one-page recipe works in any AI app. Print it and keep it by your computer.

The recipe: Who, What, How

IngredientWhat to sayExample
1. WhoWho you are, or who it should be"I'm a parent of two" or "Act as a patient cooking teacher"
2. WhatThe job, with the details that matter"Plan a birthday party for a 7-year-old, 10 kids, £60 budget"
3. HowThe shape you want back"A checklist, under 15 items, in order of when to do them"

Put together, it looks like this

I'm a parent of two and not very crafty. Plan a birthday party for a 7-year-old, 10 kids, £60 budget, at home. Give me a checklist of under 15 items, in the order I should do them.

Five magic follow-ups

  • "Shorter, please." When it waffles.
  • "Ask me three questions first." When you're not sure what you want.
  • "Give me three options." When you want to choose.
  • "Explain that more simply." When it's gone over your head.
  • "What might I have missed?" When it really matters.
Remember: it's a conversation. If the first answer isn't right, just say what to change. You don't need to start again.
