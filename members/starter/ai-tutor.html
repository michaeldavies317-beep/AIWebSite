Starter

Learn anything with AI as your tutor

A patient teacher that never gets tired of questions. Use it to learn a language, a hobby, a skill for work or something you always meant to understand.

1. Make a learning plan

I want to learn [skill]. I'm a complete beginner and have about [time] a week. Make me a simple 4-week plan with one small goal each week. Ask me two questions first so it fits me.

2. Learn in small bites

Teach me the first lesson in under 300 words, with one example. Then give me a tiny task to try, and wait for my answer before going on.

3. Get quizzed

Quiz me on what we've covered. Ask one question at a time, tell me if I'm right, and explain gently if I'm not.

4. Practise a language

Let's practise [language]. I'm a beginner. Chat with me about [everyday topic] using short, simple sentences. Correct my mistakes kindly at the end of each reply, in English.

5. Stuck on something?

I don't understand [thing]. Explain it three different ways: with an everyday comparison, step by step, and with a simple example. Then ask which one helped.

Tip: come back to the same chat each week, so it remembers where you got to. Ask it "Where did we get to?" to pick up again.

Stuck? As a member you can email support@easystartai.co.uk and we'll reply within two working days (one working day on Plus).
