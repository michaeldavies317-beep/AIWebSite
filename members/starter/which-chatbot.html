ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini? A plain-English cheat sheet

They're more alike than different. All three can write, explain, summarise and brainstorm well. The best one is usually the one you'll actually use, so pick based on where you already spend your day.

A quick way to choose

  • You live in Google (Gmail, Docs, Android): try Gemini first. It sits close to the tools you already use.
  • You want a careful writing and thinking partner: try Claude. Many people like it for longer documents and clear, calm explanations.
  • You want the one your friends and colleagues use: try ChatGPT. It's the best known, with lots of guides online.

Things that matter more than the brand

  1. Free versus paid. The free versions are great for getting started. Only pay once you're using it most days and hitting limits.
  2. Privacy settings. Look in each app's settings for whether your chats can be used to improve the service, and switch it off if you prefer.
  3. Check important facts. All of them can sound sure and still be wrong. Double-check anything about health, money or the law.

Try this in any of them

I'm new to AI. I mostly want help with [your task]. Ask me three questions, then suggest the simplest way to start.

Features and prices change often, so check each app's own site for the latest. Written November 2026.
