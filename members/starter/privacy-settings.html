Privacy settings in 5 minutes

A quick tidy-up of the settings that matter, so you feel comfortable using AI every day.

The one setting to check first

Most AI apps can use your chats to help improve their service. That's usually fine for everyday questions, but you can switch it off if you prefer. Here's roughly where to look. App menus move around, so if you can't find it, search the app's help pages for "data controls" or "privacy".

AppWhere to lookWhat it's usually called
ChatGPTYour name or picture, then Settings, then Data controls"Improve the model for everyone"
ClaudeYour initials, then Settings, then Privacy"Help improve Claude"
GeminiMenu, then Activity (in your Google account)"Gemini Apps Activity" or "Keep activity"

Four more quick checks

  1. Memory. Some apps remember facts about you between chats. Have a look at what it's saved, and delete anything you'd rather it forgot.
  2. Old chats. You can delete chats you don't need. Most apps also have a "temporary" or "incognito" chat that isn't saved.
  3. Shared links. If you've ever shared a chat with someone, check your list of shared links and remove ones you no longer need.
  4. Connected apps. If you've linked your email, calendar or files, make sure you're happy with what it can see.

A simple rule for what to type

If you wouldn't write it on a postcard, leave it out or swap it for a placeholder. For example, use "[my bank]" rather than your account number, and "my neighbour" rather than their full name.
