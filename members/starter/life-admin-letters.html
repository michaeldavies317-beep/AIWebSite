Life admin letters

Copy a prompt, fill in the [brackets], and paste it into your AI chat. You'll get a clear, polite letter or email you can send in minutes.

1. Asking for a refund

Write a polite, firm email to [shop or company] asking for a refund for [item], bought on [date] for £[amount]. The problem is [what went wrong]. I'd like [refund / replacement] within 14 days. Mention my rights under UK consumer law in plain words. Under 150 words.

2. Complaining to your energy, phone or broadband supplier

Help me write a complaint to [supplier] about [problem], which started on [date]. I've already [what you've tried]. I want [what would put it right]. Keep it calm and clear, with the key facts in a short list, and ask for a reference number.

3. Writing to the council

Write a short letter to [council name] about [issue, for example a missed bin collection or a broken street light] at [rough location]. It's been happening since [date]. Ask what they will do and by when. Friendly but businesslike.

4. Asking your landlord to fix something

Write an email to my landlord asking them to repair [problem] at [property]. I first noticed it on [date] and it's affecting [how]. Ask when someone can come round, and keep a polite, record-keeping tone.

5. Cancelling a subscription or contract

Write a short email cancelling my [service] account, reference [account number placeholder]. I want it to end on [date] and I'd like written confirmation and no further payments taken.

6. If they don't reply

I sent this message on [date] and haven't had a reply: [paste it]. Write a short, polite follow-up that mentions the first message and asks for a response within 7 days.

Tip: AI is great at the wording, but check the facts are right before you send. For anything legal or a big sum of money, Citizens Advice is free and very good.
