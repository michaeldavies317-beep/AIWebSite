Is this app OK? A one-page checklist

Your child wants a new app with AI in it. Run through these ten questions in five minutes, and you'll know whether to say yes, not yet, or yes together.

✓QuestionWhere to find out
What age is it rated for? Is my child old enough?App Store or Google Play listing; the app's terms
Who made it? Is it a company I've heard of?The developer name on the listing
Can my child chat with strangers?Reviews; try it yourself first
Does it have an AI character that acts like a friend?App description and screenshots
Does it ask for photos, location or contacts? Does it need them?The permissions it asks for when installed
Are there in-app purchases or adverts?"In-app purchases" note on the listing
Are there parental controls?The app's settings or help pages
Can I see or delete what my child has shared?Privacy settings
What do other parents say?Reviews; Common Sense Media; Internet Matters
Have I tried it myself for 10 minutes?Your own phone

So, what's the answer?

  • Yes: right age, no chat with strangers, controls you're happy with.
  • Yes, together: fine with you in the room, or on your device, for now.
  • Not yet: too old for them, chat with strangers, or an AI "friend" you're not comfortable with. Explain why, and agree when you'll look again.
