Parent bundle bonus

AI conversation cards

Sixteen questions to get the family talking about AI, sorted by age. Print, cut along the dotted lines, and pull one out at dinner or on a car journey.

Pick a card, any card!
If a robot could help you with one job, what would it be?Ages 5+
Is a computer alive? How do you know?Ages 5+
What should we never tell a computer?Ages 5+
Make up a silly question to ask AI. Do you think it will know?Ages 5+
How do you think AI learns things?Ages 7+
If AI gets something wrong, how could we find out?Ages 7+
What's the difference between a robot friend and a real friend?Ages 7+
What would you like AI to invent?Ages 7+
When is it OK to use AI for homework, and when isn't it?Ages 10+
Have you ever seen a picture online that turned out to be fake?Ages 10+
Would you tell AI a secret? Why or why not?Ages 10+
If a friend made a fake picture of someone, what would you do?Ages 10+
Do you think AI should have rules? Who should make them?Ages 12+
What jobs do you think AI will help with when you're grown up?Ages 12+
How would you know if a chatbot was trying to keep you hooked?Ages 12+
What's one thing you'd teach a grown-up about AI?Ages 12+

Tip for grown-ups: there are no wrong answers. Ask "What makes you think that?" and share your own answer too.
