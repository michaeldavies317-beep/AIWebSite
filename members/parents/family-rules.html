Parent guide

Family AI rules poster

Agree these together, then print this page and pop it on the fridge. Use your browser's Print option.

  1. We use AI together first. Grown-ups try new AI apps with us before we use them alone.
  2. We keep our private stuff private. No full names, addresses, school names, passwords or photos of us in chats.
  3. AI helps with homework, it doesn't do it. It can explain and quiz us, but the work is ours.
  4. AI can be wrong. If something sounds odd or important, we check with a grown-up or a trusted website.
  5. If something feels weird, we say so. We can always tell a grown-up, and we won't be in trouble for asking.

Want help setting up family controls? See the Family set-up guide, or email parents@easystartai.co.uk.
